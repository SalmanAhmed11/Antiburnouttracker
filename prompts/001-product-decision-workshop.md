# Anti Burnout — Product Rules Review Workshop

We are **not coding**. Work from `AGENTS.md`, `docs/product/PRODUCT_BASELINE.md`, and `docs/product/DECISION_REGISTER.md`; consult the original master only if directly available.

Review PD-01 to PD-05 first, then later rounds with approval. For each issue:
1. Reproduce the existing locked principle exactly.
2. Describe the undecided part without mixing in old unapproved brainstorming.
3. Offer two or three clear alternatives with engineering, UX, fairness and security tradeoffs.
4. Recommend a starting beta rule and explain why, with at least two edge-case scenarios.
5. Record **owner approval or rejection**. Do not invent it.
6. Specify exact acceptance examples and updates needed in screen designs.

At the end provide the revised decision register diff for review, and stop. Do not generate Flutter, Supabase schemas, API implementations or deployment changes.
